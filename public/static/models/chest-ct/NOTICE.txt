HealthcareDL chest CT research artifacts

Dataset and two reencoded validation images:
SARS-CoV-2 CT-scan dataset: A large dataset of real patients CT scans for SARS-CoV-2 identification (2020).
Eduardo Soares, Plamen Angelov, Sarah Biaso, Michele Higa Froes, Daniel Kanda Abe.
https://doi.org/10.1101/2020.04.24.20078584
https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/SZDUQX
Current release: A large dataset of real patients CT scans for COVID-19 identification.
Publisher-declared dataset license: CC0 1.0
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: exact duplicate images removed before splitting; images resized and normalized for CNN input; two validation images reencoded as PNG. A classification head was trained for this project. No medical claim or endorsement by the dataset authors is implied. Patient folders are kept separate across partitions. Preserve source attribution and these notices; backbone weights and other inherited materials retain their own terms.

Backbone: ImageNet-pretrained MobileNetV3 Small from Torchvision, using a frozen encoder.
Torchvision source software license: BSD-3-Clause, reproduced in TORCHVISION-LICENSE.txt. Pretrained weights and source data retain their upstream terms.
https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models/generated/torchvision.models.mobilenet_v3_small.html

Execution: ONNX Runtime Web, MIT license and third-party notices in ../../vendor/onnx/.

This is a two-pattern, image-level research model, not a diagnostic service, complete radiology interpretation, or clinically validated medical device. Comparator does not mean healthy or COVID-negative. See report.json and the repository model documentation for the measured evaluation and its limitations.
