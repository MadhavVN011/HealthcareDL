PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

Your inputs, explained.

This educational application processes the symptom names you submit to generate a response. The application does not store submissions in a database, write them to application logs, use analytics or advertising trackers, set tracking cookies, or use submissions to retrain the model.

Theme preference

The light/dark switch saves only your theme choice in this browser's local storage, under healthcaredl-theme. It is not sent to the server. Clearing this site's browser data removes the preference. Symptom submissions are not saved in local storage.

Blood reports and CT images

Report extraction, English OCR, and chest CT classification run in your browser. Selected files, extracted text, and image pixels are not uploaded to this application or an external AI service, are not used for training, and are not saved in local storage, IndexedDB, or a database by the app. Model and language files are fetched from this site; the OCR language cache is disabled. Clear the workspace or close the tab when finished. Browser memory, extensions, operating-system activity, and screenshots are outside the app's control.

Hosting and request metadata

Your hosting provider may process IP addresses, request paths, device information, and operational logs. Hosting retention and access settings depend on the deployed environment. Application responses use no-store headers, but the application cannot control your browser history, screenshots, extensions, network infrastructure, or all provider logs.

Optional voice input

Voice recognition is performed through your browser. Your browser or its speech provider may transmit and process microphone audio. The app requests no microphone access until you activate voice input. You can always type or select symptoms instead.

External educational links

MedlinePlus and repository links open external services governed by their own policies. The application sends no symptom query in those links and uses no-referrer link handling.

Use example data for demonstrations

This service does not provide clinical care. Avoid entering identifiable information or other people's health details. No compliance certification or clinical privacy guarantee is claimed. For software questions, use the repository's issue tracker without including personal health information.

Updated November 11, 2026. This notice describes application behavior; the deployment operator must separately establish provider retention, access controls, and applicable obligations.
